# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. For detail, follow the links.

## Is ResQ Bharat an official warning system?

No. It is a **decision-support tool** that combines satellite data, weather
forecasts, and AI. Always follow official warnings and call **112** in an emergency.

## Can it tell me if my area is flooding right now?

No. It has **no live river gauges**. It estimates **risk** from rainfall and flood-
proneness, and shows **where** flooding is historically likely — not live flood
status.

## What does the risk level mean?

It's a combined estimate of flood risk (Low / Medium / High / Severe) from the
area's susceptibility and recent + forecast rainfall. It is **not** a calibrated
probability. See [Flood intelligence](flood-intelligence.md).

## Why is one area "validated" and another "modelled"?

Validated areas have been checked against real past floods using radar satellites;
modelled areas are computed from datasets but not individually validated. Trust
validated areas more. See
[Methodology & data quality](methodology-and-data-quality.md).

## Are the crop-loss and damage rupee figures accurate?

They are **indicative estimates** using documented assumptions, shown with ranges —
useful for planning, not for accounting or insurance.

## Does "new construction" mean illegal building?

No. It flags **candidates** where satellites suggest new building near rivers. Always
verify on the ground. See [Floodplain development](floodplain-development.md).

## How many areas are covered?

**100 study areas across India** (28 states and 8 union territories), organised by
river basin.

## What languages are supported?

The interface supports ten languages; the AI assistant can answer in more. Voice
input/read-aloud depend on your device. See
[Multilingual & voice](multilingual-and-voice.md).

## Is my data private?

Reports are stored, and name/contact are optional — only share what you're
comfortable with. Uploaded photos are processed by the AI model. See
[Security & privacy](../technical/security-and-privacy.md).

## Why do some panels show "unavailable"?

The app couldn't compute that layer (e.g. too much cloud for a clean image). It
prefers honesty over guessing. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Does submitting a report call for rescue?

No. It records a hazard for awareness. For rescue, call **112**. See
[Reports & response](reports-and-response.md).

## Can I trust the AI answers completely?

Treat them as **grounded guidance**, not official instructions. The AI is told not
to invent live data, alerts, or phone numbers. See
[Gemini AI help](gemini-ai-help.md).

## Who is this for?

Citizens, volunteers, NGOs, and local responders who want an accessible, multilingual
view of flood risk across India.
